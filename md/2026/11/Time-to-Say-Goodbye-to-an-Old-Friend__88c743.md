## Introduction

This article documents some of my recent activities.

Unlike my previous articles, this one doesn’t introduce any vulnerabilities or offensive techniques. Instead, I want to share some thoughts about what I’ve been up to recently. You can think of it as a monthly diary.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zhiyu-zeng/img@main/img/2026/10/23c0c19572c15f21.png)

## Certified

About a week ago, I was dealing with something related to my future. Before that, I had been receiving one piece of disappointing news after another, which made me constantly question my abilities in cybersecurity and the value of my other efforts.

Then, all of a sudden, I received some good news!

Of course, it also brought along some new uncertainties, but at least it gave me some confidence.

That night, I was browsing YouTube, looking for a nice video to watch. I even bought a bag of snacks, hoping to enjoy the moment.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zhiyu-zeng/img@main/img/2026/10/bfe0c4c8cdc8f962.png)

And then… my laptop suddenly shut down.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zhiyu-zeng/img@main/img/2026/11/0e20987b88df78b8.png)

It didn’t respond at all, no matter whether I plugged in the power adapter or kept pressing the power button.

There’s a saying: “When God closes a door, He opens a window.”

In my case, however, I’d like to put it this way:

“When you finally get a fire going, God floods your farm.”

Anyway, after four days of investigation, my laptop was officially pronounced dead…

## My Old Friend

I bought this laptop a long time ago. It came with Windows 10.

I still remember the first day I saw it in a computer store and decided to buy it.

I still remember that the first things I did after bringing it home were installing Chrome, Visual Studio, Visual Studio Code, and Python.

Then, I wrote my first Python RAT (in case you wonder, you can find it here: [PyShell](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/IssacTools/blob/main/PyShell.zip)).

It was my only friend when I became an international student.

I used it for everything: doing homework, researching, playing video games, texting, sending emails, coding, and pentesting.

It malfunctioned several times over the years, but I managed to get it fixed every time. However, this time, it really is dead.

I have no choice but to purchase a new laptop to replace my old friend and continue pursuing my dreams.

During these years as an international student, I struggled with depression and couldn’t find a way out of the abyss created by my chaotic mindset.

Whenever I felt depressed and broke down crying, I would spend time with my laptop.

Starting in January this year, I began trying to get out of that state.

I kept learning and coding.

I published [DuplexSpy](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/DuplexSpyCS/), [Eden-RAT](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/Eden-RAT/), [dotNetPELoader](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/dotNetPELoader), [IronPE](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/IronPE), [Elfina](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/Elfina), [OpenBootloader](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/OpenBootloader), [OpenPetya](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/OpenPetya), [Alien](https://github.com/iss4cf0ng/Alien), and more.

I continued improving my skills with Metasploit, Ghidra, IDA, and WinDbg.

I also started several blog series and kept updating them, including [Inside Different Generations of RATs](https://iss4cf0ng.github.io/InsideDifferentGenerationsOfRATs/), [Inside Different Generations of Ransomware](https://iss4cf0ng.github.io/InsideDifferentGenerationsOfRansomware/), [From Bug To Exploit](https://iss4cf0ng.github.io/FromBugToExploit/), and more.

I learned about stack-based buffer overflow attacks, progressing from simply modifying the execution flow to Return-Oriented Programming (ROP).

I also started learning driver development, and I will keep working to improve my skills in this area.

## Guilt and Motivation

The root cause of the malfunction was that the CPU had failed. Furthermore, I think I know exactly why it happened.

A few days before the CPU failed, I could hear a screw rattling around inside the laptop. It seems that the loose screw may have caused a short circuit somewhere inside.

In other words, it was my fault.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zhiyu-zeng/img@main/img/2026/10/23c0c19572c15f21.png)

I feel guilty.

When I was a little child, I used to think I was a cold-blooded person. I rarely felt anything when someone got injured in front of me. I also couldn’t understand why violinists were so emotionally attached to their violins, or why they would get angry if someone else touched them.

Now, I think I understand.

My laptop is not just a machine to me. It is a true companion. When someone touches it without my permission, I get angry too.

It carried me through some of the toughest times in my life…

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zhiyu-zeng/img@main/img/2026/09/b730ebad7dc0dda9.jpg)

Before putting it away, I gave it a hug and rested my forehead against it. It might sound silly, but that moment reminded me of the very first day I bought it, as well as all those times when I broke down crying in front of it because of the difficulties I was facing.

I no longer want to pretend to be cold-blooded just to make myself look like a mature adult.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zhiyu-zeng/img@main/img/2026/09/f6caba92d2a1dfb1.jpg)

I’ll keep it. I don’t want to sell it or throw it away.

And I will keep learning.

When I was a child, I learned things just to show off. During my school years, I had no idea why I had to study other than to pass exams.

But now, I want to learn for myself and to achieve my dreams.

And if I ever become capable enough, I would love to repair my own computer someday.

## Conclusion

This is the end of the article.

Perhaps I let my emotions get the better of me, and the structure of this article is a little messy.

But I guess that’s fine. This is, after all, a diary.
