We’re honored that Microsoft has once again been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Security Information and Event Management (SIEM).

As security environments grow more complex and attackers move faster, organizations need more than the ability to collect and analyze data. They need comprehensive visibility, context to understand what is happening, and the ability to act before an attack causes further damage.

[Explore Microsoft Sentinel SIEM capabilities](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/business/siem-and-xdr/microsoft-sentinel-siem)

## Comprehensive SIEM across the digital estate

[Microsoft Sentinel](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/business/siem-and-xdr/microsoft-sentinel-siem) is our cloud-native SIEM, bringing together security data, advanced analytics, automation, and threat intelligence to help organizations protect their digital estate.

With more than 500 connectors across Microsoft and third-party solutions, Microsoft Sentinel brings together signals from across complex, multicloud environments. Its rich library of out-of-the-box detections, informed by Microsoft’s threat intelligence and organizational context, helps defenders identify sophisticated threats without building every detection from scratch.

Microsoft Sentinel supports the core capabilities security teams depend on, from scalable security data management and advanced detection to threat hunting, investigation, and response. The Microsoft Sentinel data lake centralizes historical and real-time security data across platforms and clouds for cost-effective retention and advanced analytics at scale. Security operations center (SOC) optimization and cost management help teams improve coverage, identify detection gaps, and make informed decisions about the data they collect and retain.

## Reimagining security operations for the agentic era

On September 23, 2026, [Microsoft introduced](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/09/23/reimagining-the-soc-for-the-agentic-era-in-microsoft-defender/?msockid=0139329c3fb06e3a084e266d3e666fc6) the **[Integrated Security Operations Center (ISOC)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/business/solutions/integrated-security-operations-center) in Microsoft Defender**, reimagining security operations for the agentic era. As threats grow more sophisticated and security environments more complex, traditional approaches built on disconnected tools and workflows can no longer keep pace. ISOC brings security operations and threat protection together, creating a unified foundation where people and AI agents can see, understand, and act across the environment.

Through deep integration with Microsoft Sentinel, ISOC combines the full breadth of SIEM capabilities with extended detection and response (XDR), threat intelligence, posture management, and response. By connecting signals from Microsoft and third-party environments with rich security context and coordinated action, ISOC enables security teams to move beyond fragmented investigations toward faster, more proactive protection. Rather than operating as separate tools, the security stack works as one system, providing the foundation for agentic security.

**Attack disruption demonstrates what this integrated approach makes possible**. Microsoft Defender correlates signals across Microsoft and third-party environments with threat intelligence to identify high-confidence attacks in progress and automatically contain compromised users and devices before threats spread. This includes ransomware, business email compromise, and adversary-in-the-middle attacks. Across customers, ransomware attacks are disrupted in just **30 seconds on average**.

Together, these capabilities demonstrate how Microsoft is moving security operations beyond detection and response toward an integrated, agentic approach that helps organizations anticipate, investigate, and stop threats faster.

## A shared foundation for people and agents

AI is becoming part of how security teams work every day. Microsoft Sentinel combines advanced analytics, machine learning, and automation to help analysts use natural language to hunt for threats, generate response playbooks, support alert triage, analyze complex signals, and accelerate investigations. These capabilities reduce manual work and give defenders more time to focus on the threats and decisions that matter most.

By enabling SIEM and native protection to operate as one system, ISOC gives people and AI agents a shared foundation to see, understand, and act across the environment. Agents bring speed and scale to continuous security operations, while people provide expertise, judgment, and accountability.

Together, they can move beyond traditional, linear workflows toward continuous detection, investigation, and attack disruption. As agents take on more security operations workflows, comprehensive data, and rich context, the ability to act becomes even more important to helping organizations stop threats faster and deliver better security outcomes.

## Looking ahead

The future of SIEM is about more than managing security data. It’s about ensuring your SIEM is a core building block of the agentic SOC, bringing together security data, intelligence, AI, and native protection so people and agents can understand threats sooner, act faster, and stop attacks before they spread.

We’ll continue investing in the comprehensive SIEM capabilities customers rely on while advancing integrated, agentic security operations in Microsoft Defender. We’re grateful for the Gartner recognition and for the customers, partners, and security community whose feedback continues to shape Microsoft Sentinel.
