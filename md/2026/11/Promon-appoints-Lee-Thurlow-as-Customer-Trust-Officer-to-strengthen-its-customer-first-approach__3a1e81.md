OSLO, Norway – November 19, 2026 – Promon, whose Runtime Intelligence Platform protects more than 2 billion app installs worldwide, today announced the appointment of Lee Thurlow as Customer Trust Officer. Thurlow, who joined Promon on October 12, 2026, will also serve as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and Data Protection Officer (DPO).
