### RandoriSec 1 min

RandoriSec has obtained the PASSI qualification from the ANSSI.

For more than ten years, RandoriSec has developed expertise in pentest and security audits across a wide range of environments and technologies. This qualification marks the culmination of several months of work by our teams. The PASSI qualification validates our audit and penetration testing methodology, as well as our organizational rigor, in accordance with ANSSI requirements.

We know that attacks are becoming increasingly significant and that every organization must be able to rely on trusted service providers to help secure their environments. For our clients, this new milestone provides an additional guarantee regarding the technical expertise of our auditors and the rigor of our audit processes.

We would also like to thank our teams who contributed to obtaining this certification!

For any inquiries regarding our certified audits, contact our team: [**sales@randorisec.fr**](mailto:sales@randorisec.fr)
